Marinated Flank Steak

A great flank steak marinade like this one is important if you want a tender, juicy, flavorful steak. Make sure you marinate your flank steak for at least 2 hours for best results or longer if you have time. This wonderful quick and easy recipe also works great when the steak is sliced and used for fajitas.

Flank steak is a lean cut of beef that comes from the cow’s lower abdominal muscles. It’s a relatively tough cut that’s low in fat, which means a few things: Flank steak doesn’t need to be trimmed, it’s inexpensive compared to other cuts, and it benefits greatly from marination.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • ⅓ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • ¼ cup red wine vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 ½ tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 (1 1/2) pound flank steak

Steps

  1. Whisk oil, soy sauce, vinegar, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, garlic, and pepper together in a medium bowl until well combined. Place steak in a 9x13-inch glass baking dish.
  2. Pour marinade over flank steak; turn several times to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 2 to 6 hours, or up to 12 hours, if you have time.
  3. When ready to cook, preheat an outdoor grill for medium-high heat and lightly oil the grate
  4. Remove steak from marinade and shake off excess. Discard remaining marinade.
  5. Cook steak on the preheated grill about 5 minutes per side, or to desired doneness.
  6. Remove from the grill and let rest for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
